Services

Websites, AI automation and consulting, built around how your business works

Ostendic works in four areas. They often work together, but each stands on its own.

01

Websites and Digital Presence

Websites and web apps that explain what you do, work well on a phone, and make it easy to get in touch or buy.

You need a new website, a better one, or a web app your customers use.
The problem

Business websites, landing pages, online stores, Shopify and WordPress sites, custom web apps, redesigns.

02

AI Automation and Intelligent Agents

Workflow automation and custom AI agents that take repetitive work off your team, with people approving what matters.

More on AI Automation
Who it helps
Teams stuck repeating manual steps: answering enquiries, chasing leads, copying data between tools.
The problem
Leads wait for a reply. Questions pile up. Someone spends hours moving data from one tool to another.
What we do
Enquiry and lead follow-up workflows, reporting automation, tool integrations and custom AI agents.
A project can include
  • Mapping where time gets lost
  • Designing it, including where a person approves
  • Building it and connecting it to your tools
  • Testing on real cases before going live

An example: following up new enquiries

  1. 01

    A new enquiry arrives through your website form or email.

  2. 02

    An agent reads it, pulls out the key details and adds them to your CRM or spreadsheet.

  3. 03

    It drafts a reply using your own service and pricing information, and passes it to your team to approve.

  4. 04

    If the customer hasn’t replied after a set time, it prepares a follow-up and reminds the right person.

An illustration of the kind of workflow we build, not a client case.

03

AI Agent Development

Custom AI agents that handle routine cases inside your tools, within limits you set.

More on AI Agent Development
Who it helps
Teams with a steady flow of requests, documents or records that need reading and a first decision.
The problem
Fixed-rule automations break when a case doesn’t fit, and the work falls back on people.
What we do
Operations agents, enquiry and support agents, multi-agent systems and approval flows.
A project can include
  • Defining the agent’s job and limits
  • Building it and connecting it to your tools
  • Testing on real past cases
  • Monitoring after launch
04

Business Strategy and Consulting

Strategy-led consulting that finds what’s slowing the business down and sets out a clear, prioritised plan.

More on Business Consulting
Who it helps
Founders and growing businesses that want an outside view of where growth is stuck.
The problem
Enquiries get lost, work depends on a few people, and there are too many priorities at once.
What we do
Growth assessments, process improvement, digital transformation plans, technology and AI adoption.
A project can include
  • Reviewing how you win and serve customers
  • Finding the bottlenecks
  • A written, prioritised plan
  • Help putting it in place
