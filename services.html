FAQ

Questions about our services

01

Which service should I start with?

Start with the task that costs you the most time or the most missed business. If that’s enquiries, an AI agent or AI automation usually comes first. If you’re not sure, a short consulting call will map it out.

02

Can I combine more than one service?

Yes, and many projects do. A website that captures enquiries, an automation that sorts them and an AI agent that replies can all be scoped as one project.

03

Do I have to change the tools I already use?

Usually not. We connect the CRM, inbox, forms, spreadsheets and WhatsApp you already use, as long as they allow connections.

04

How is the price worked out?

Every project is scoped and priced in writing before any work starts, based on what it needs to do. You know the cost, and what’s included, up front.

05

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in India and abroad, by video call, email and WhatsApp.
