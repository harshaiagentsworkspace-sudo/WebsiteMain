Services

AI automation first, and websites built to match

Ostendic works in five areas. Four focus on AI automation and integration; the fifth is website design and development. Each can stand on its own or work together.

01

AI Automation & Workflow Solutions

Automate repetitive processes, connect business tools, and reduce operational friction.

Explore AI Automation
Who it helps
Teams that repeat the same manual steps every day: answering enquiries, following up leads, copying data between tools.
The problem
Work waits between people and tools. Leads wait for a reply, and hours go into moving information by hand.
What we do
Enquiry and lead workflows, data entry between tools, reporting automation and integrations, with AI where it helps.
A project can include
  • Mapping where time is lost today
  • Designing the workflow, including approvals
  • Building it and connecting your tools
  • Testing on real cases before launch

An example: following up new enquiries

  1. 01

    A new enquiry arrives through your website form or email.

  2. 02

    An agent reads it, pulls out the key details and adds them to your CRM or spreadsheet.

  3. 03

    It drafts a reply using your own service and pricing information, and passes it to your team to approve.

  4. 04

    If the customer hasn’t replied after a set time, it prepares a follow-up and reminds the right person.

An illustration of the kind of workflow we build, not a client case.

02

Custom AI Agent Development

Develop intelligent agents that assist with business workflows, information retrieval, customer interactions, and suitable multi-step tasks.

Explore Custom AI Agent Development
Who it helps
Teams with a steady flow of requests, documents or questions that need reading and a first decision.
The problem
Fixed-rule automations break when a case doesn’t fit the pattern, and the work falls back on people.
What we do
Enquiry and support agents, internal knowledge assistants, operations agents and agents that prepare work for approval.
A project can include
  • Defining the agent’s job and limits
  • Connecting it to your tools and information
  • Testing on real past cases
  • Monitoring and refinement after launch

Workflow Automation

Connect your tools and automate repetitive, rule-based processes, with error handling and monitoring built in.

Explore Workflow Automation

Businesses where information is re-typed between forms, inboxes, CRMs and spreadsheets.
The problem
Tools don’t talk to each other, so people bridge the gaps by hand, and details slip through.
What we do
Workflow mapping, tool integrations, lead routing, reporting automation and administrative workflows.
A project can include
  • Mapping the process step by step
  • Connecting the tools involved
  • Error handling and alerts
  • Monitoring after go-live

AI Strategy & Integration
