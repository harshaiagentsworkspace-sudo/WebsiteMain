Client projectHealthcare e-commerce · India

VMS Careline

What it is
A healthcare e-commerce business in India, restructured into two clear digital verticals: VMS Careline and VMS Cart.
The problem
One digital presence carried two quite different offerings, so customers had to work out which part of the business they were in. Enquiries were handled manually, and how quickly someone got a reply depended on who was available.
What was built
Separate verticals, each with its own audience, structure and buying path. A commerce experience rebuilt around how these products are actually chosen, with clearer product discovery and presentation. An automated enquiry path from first contact through first response to follow-up.
Scope
Brand structure, UI/UX design, Website development, E-commerce, Enquiry automation
Who built it
Client project. Ostendic, led by Harsh Dubey, handled the brand structure, UX, website development and enquiry automation.
Visit vmscareline.com

Demo buildWeb application · AI analysis

TrueReview

What it is
A free tool that checks the reviews on a product listing before you buy.
The problem
Shoppers can’t easily tell which product reviews to trust, or spot the complaints buried under hundreds of five-star ratings.
What was built
Product concept, interface design and front end, plus an AI review flow. You paste a product link or name, and the tool scans its reviews for likely fakes, repeated patterns and buried complaints, then gives a trust score with a breakdown.
Who built it
Designed and built in-house by Ostendic as a demonstration product.

Demo buildWeb application · AI review

ViralCheck

What it is
A free tool that reviews a piece of written content (a post, article or newsletter) before it is published.
The problem
Creators and small marketing teams usually find out whether a post works after publishing it, when it’s too late to change.
What was built
Interface design and front end, plus an AI review flow. You paste the content, choose the platform, and get feedback on hook strength, readability, SEO gaps and call-to-action clarity, with suggested rewrites.
Who built it
Designed and built in-house by Ostendic as a demonstration product.

Demo buildWeb application · AI analysis

LaunchMirror

What it is
A free web tool that gives Indian founders a structured first read on a business idea before they commit to building it.
The problem
First-time founders often start building before testing whether an idea holds up. Market timing, competition and cost get checked late, if at all.
What was built
Product concept, interface design and front end, plus an AI analysis flow. The founder answers seven questions and receives a viability score out of 100, a market check, short statements on strengths, weaknesses and risk, and a 90-day action plan.
Who built it
Designed and built in-house by Ostendic as a demonstration product.
