Client project

VMS Careline

Brand structure, e-commerce experience and enquiry handling rebuilt across two business verticals.

SectorHealthcare e-commerce
MarketIndia
Year2026
ScopeBrand, UI/UX Design, Website Development, E-commerce, Enquiry Automation
01 — Before

One undifferentiated digital presence carried two quite different offerings. Customers arriving for either had to work out which part of the business they were in. Enquiries were handled manually, and time to first response depended on who was available.

02 — Approach

Separate the business into distinct digital verticals — VMS Careline and VMS Cart — each with its own audience, structure and buying path. Rebuild the commerce experience around how these products are actually chosen, and automate the enquiry path so response time stops depending on availability.

03 — Delivered

A healthcare e-commerce experience with clearer product discovery and stronger presentation, a separate cart vertical, and an automated path from enquiry through first response to follow-up.
