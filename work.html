Work

What we’ve built, and what it does

VMS Careline

What it is
The website and brand structure for VMS Careline, a healthcare e-commerce business in India.
The problem
Two kinds of customers, one tangled site. Products were hard to find, and enquiries had no clear path to a reply.
What was built
Two separate verticals: VMS Careline and VMS Cart. A store built around how people choose products. An automated path from first enquiry to follow-up.
Visit the live site

TrueReview

What it is
A demo tool that helps shoppers decide if a product is worth buying.
The problem
Online reviews are hard to trust, so it’s hard to know what’s actually worth buying.
What was built
A working tool where you ask about a product and get a score based on its genuine reviews.

LaunchMirror

What it is
A demo tool that gives you a structured first read on a business idea before you commit to building it.
The problem
It’s easy to start building before you’ve checked whether an idea holds up. Market timing, competition and cost often get checked late, if at all.
What was built
A working tool where you answer seven questions about your idea. You get a viability score out of 100, a market check, a short read on strengths, weaknesses and risks, and a 90-day action plan.
