Services

Two things we do, and do properly

01

Web Development and UI/UX Design

Websites and web apps that explain what you do, work well on a phone, and make it easy to get in touch or buy.

More on web development and UI/UX
Who it helps
You need a new website, a better one, or a web app your customers use.
The problem
Visitors can’t tell what you do, or how to get in touch. So they leave.
What we build
Business websites, landing pages, online stores, Shopify and WordPress sites, custom web apps, redesigns.
A project can include
  • Planning the pages and the visitor’s path
  • Design for phone, tablet and desktop
  • A custom build, or WordPress and Shopify
  • Testing before launch
02

Agentic AI Workflows

AI workflows that connect your tools and take the repetitive work off your team.

More on agentic AI workflows
Who it helps
Teams stuck repeating manual steps: answering enquiries, chasing leads, copying data between tools.
The problem
Leads wait for a reply. Questions pile up. Someone spends hours moving data from one tool to another.
What we build
AI agents for business operations, lead follow-up, support workflows, process automation, tool integrations.
A project can include
  • Mapping where time gets lost
  • Designing it, including where a person approves
  • Building it and connecting it to your tools
  • Testing on real cases before going live

An example: following up new enquiries

  1. New enquiry arrives
  2. Agent logs the details
  3. Reply drafted, team approves
  4. Follow-up prepared, person reminded

An illustration of the kind of workflow we build, not a client case.
