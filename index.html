The challenge

What usually slows a business down

Most of the businesses we talk to are dealing with one or more of these.

  • 01

    Not enough customers getting in touch

    People find you, look around and leave. The enquiries that should be coming in aren’t.

  • 02

    A weak online presence

    A website that doesn’t explain what you do, or is awkward on a phone, quietly turns visitors away.

  • 03

    Repetitive admin

    Hours go into copying details between tools, answering the same questions and chasing follow-ups.

  • 04

    Workflows that leak time

    Work waits between people and tools, and too much depends on someone remembering to do it.

  • 05

    No clear growth plan

    There are plenty of ideas, but no agreed view of what to fix first or what will actually move the business forward.

FAQ

Questions businesses ask us

01

What does Ostendic do?

Ostendic helps businesses grow and run more smoothly in four ways: websites and web apps, AI automation for repetitive work, custom AI agents, and practical business consulting. Each can be used on its own or together.

02

How can AI automation help a small business?

It takes on the repetitive steps that eat into a small team’s day: logging and answering enquiries, following up leads, copying details between tools and preparing reports. People still approve anything that matters.

03

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely, so we work with businesses in India and in other countries.

04

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

No. We explain things in plain language, handle the technical work, and hand over notes your team can follow.

05

How do I know which service I need?

Tell us what’s slowing the business down. If it’s how customers find and contact you, that usually points to the website. If it’s repetitive work, automation or an agent. If you’re not sure where the problem is, consulting is the place to start. We’ll tell you honestly if we’re not the right fit.

Not enough enquiries? Too many repetitive tasks? A growth strategy that isn’t delivering? Tell us what you’re dealing with, and we’ll help you explore a practical next step.
