Automations in action

Automations That Work While You’re Busy.

Three examples of the workflows we build, from a simple email follow-up to an AI voice agent that phones every new enquiry within a minute. Pick one and press run.

Every enquiry gets a call back in under a minute.

Most enquiries go cold while they wait for a reply. Here an AI voice agent phones each new lead straight away, answers their questions from your own information and books them in, while your team gets on with the work in front of them.

  1. 01

    Every channel, one flow

    Website forms, lead ads and missed calls start the same workflow. Duplicates are removed and each lead is saved to your CRM.

  2. 02

    Called straight away

    Inside your opening hours, the AI voice agent calls the lead immediately. Outside them, it waits and calls as soon as you open.

  3. 03

    A real conversation

    It introduces itself as an AI assistant, answers from your own prices and FAQs, qualifies the lead and offers free slots from your calendar.

  4. 04

    The right next step

    Booked calls get a confirmation message. Warm leads go to your sales team, unanswered calls are retried, and the rest are updated in the CRM.

  5. 05

    Logged and summarised

    Every call is saved with a transcript and a short summary, and you get a note of what happened.

A Workflow That Follows Up, So Nobody Has To.

A simpler one, and a good place to start: it emails new leads, reads the replies and sends the next message on its own, so no lead is forgotten.

  1. 01

    Runs on a schedule

    Every hour on working days, it reads your lead list from a spreadsheet.

  2. 02

    Writes the first email

    New leads get an email personalised with AI from the details you hold, then sent from your own inbox.

  3. 03

    Watches for replies

    It checks each email thread. When someone replies, your team is told and the sequence stops.

  4. 04

    Follows up and logs it

    No reply after the wait you set? It sends the next email in the same thread and records when.

Your week in numbers, written up before Monday starts.

Sales, enquiries and call outcomes collected from your tools every Monday morning, then turned by AI into a short plain-English report sent to your phone and inbox.

  1. 01

    Gathers the numbers

    At 8 am every Monday it reads last week’s sales, enquiries and call outcomes from the tools you already use.

  2. 02

    Writes the summary

    An AI model turns the figures into a short report and compares them with the week before.

  3. 03

    Sends it to you

    The summary arrives as a chat message, with the full report attached as a PDF by email.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

01

What is AI automation for businesses?

AI automation combines workflow automation, which moves information between tools and triggers the next step, with AI models that can read, classify, summarise or draft text. Together they can handle the routine parts of a process, such as sorting enquiries or preparing reports, while people handle the parts that need judgement.

02

How can AI help reduce repetitive work?

By taking on steps that follow a pattern: reading and sorting incoming messages, extracting details from emails or documents, drafting standard replies, updating records and assembling regular reports. How much time this saves depends on the process, so we look at the actual work before estimating anything.

03

What types of business tasks can be automated?

Good candidates happen often, follow similar steps each time and mostly involve moving or reading information: enquiry handling, lead routing, follow-up reminders, data entry between tools, scheduled reports and answering common questions from your own documents. Tasks that need careful judgement on every case usually keep a person in charge.

04

Do I need to replace existing software?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use, such as your CRM, inbox, forms and spreadsheets, wherever they offer an API or a supported integration. If a tool can’t be connected, we explain the options before anything is built.

05

What is the difference between AI agents and traditional automation?

Traditional automation follows fixed rules: the same input always leads to the same steps. An AI agent uses a language model to interpret each case and choose its next step from a set of actions you allow. Agents suit work that varies from case to case; fixed automation is simpler and more predictable when the steps never change. Many solutions use both.

06

Can small businesses benefit from AI automation?

Often, yes. In a small team, repetitive tasks take time from the same few people who also serve customers. Automating one or two time-consuming tasks is usually a sensible first step, and it doesn’t need technical staff on your side.

07

Can Ostendic integrate AI with existing tools?

Yes, where the tools support it. We work with the integrations and APIs your software provides, and check this before proposing a solution. We also explain which AI provider would process your data and what it costs to run.

08

Does Ostendic also develop websites?

Yes. Website design and development is our second area of work: responsive, SEO-friendly business websites with a clear structure and a straightforward way to get in touch.

09
