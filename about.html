About

Practical AI for businesses that want to work smarter.

Ostendic builds AI automations, AI agents and websites that take repetitive work off your team and turn more enquiries into customers.

Good businesses lose hours every week to work a computer could do: answering the same questions, chasing leads, copying details from one tool into another. We build automations and AI agents around how you already work, and websites that make it easy for customers to say yes.

We’re founder-led and small by design. You talk to the person building your system, every project is scoped and priced in writing, and you end up with something your team can actually run.

Harsh DubeyFounder, Ostendic

How we work

Four things we hold to on every project

  • Start with the business problem

    Before we talk about tools, we want to know what’s slowing you down and what a good result looks like for you.

  • Say what it costs before starting

    You get the scope and the price in writing before any work starts, including what isn’t included.

  • Build what you can keep running

    We pick tools your team can live with after launch, and hand over everything you need to run them.

  • Keep people in the loop

    Automation handles the routine steps. Anything that needs judgement stays with a person.
