About

Ostendic is run by Harsh Dubey

When you work with Ostendic, you work with Harsh directly, from the first conversation through to launch.

The studio covers both sides of how a business shows up online: the website or product customers use, and the workflows that handle what happens after they get in touch.

It is a deliberately small studio. There is no account manager between you and the person designing and building your project.

How we work

Four things we hold to on every project

Start with the business problem
Before choosing a platform, a design direction or an AI tool, we want to understand what isn’t working and what a good result would look like for you.
Say what it costs before starting
Every piece of work is scoped and priced in writing before it begins, including what is not included.
Build what you can keep running
We choose platforms and tools your team can live with after launch, and hand over what you need to manage them.
Keep people in the loop
In AI workflows, routine steps can be automated. Decisions that need judgement stay with a person.
