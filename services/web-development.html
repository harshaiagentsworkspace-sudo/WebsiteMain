FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01

Do you build custom websites, or use WordPress and Shopify?

Both. A custom build suits sites with specific needs or a web application. WordPress or Shopify suits businesses that want to update pages or products themselves. We recommend one at the start and explain why.

02

Will the website work properly on phones?

Yes. Most visitors arrive on a phone, so we design for that screen first and test on phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

03

Can we update the website ourselves after launch?

Yes. We hand over the logins and notes you need, and choose a platform your team can manage.

04

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We review what the current site does well and where visitors drop off, then make the changes that make it clearer, faster and easier to use.

05

Do you also handle SEO or digital marketing?

We build sites with search engines in mind: clear page titles and descriptions, fast pages, a sitemap and a clean structure. We don’t currently run ongoing SEO or advertising campaigns.

In more detail

For those who like the full picture

Built to turn visitors into enquiries

A business website has one main job: help the right visitors understand what you offer and make it easy for them to get in touch. We plan each page around the questions visitors arrive with, place clear calls to action where they are needed and keep forms short.

Technical SEO foundations

Every site we build includes the technical groundwork search engines expect: a logical heading structure, unique page titles and descriptions, clean URLs, canonical tags, an XML sitemap, structured data where it applies, and compressed images. We don’t currently run ongoing SEO or advertising campaigns, but the site is ready for them.

Performance and responsive design

Most visitors arrive on a phone, often on a mobile connection. We design for small screens first, keep pages light, load images and video only when needed, and test on real devices and browsers before launch.

Integrations

A website can connect to the tools you already use: enquiry forms that reach your inbox or CRM, booking calendars, WhatsApp, analytics and e-commerce platforms. Where it helps, a website can also feed an automation, for example routing each new enquiry to the right person.
