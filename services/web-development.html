Service 01

Web Development and UI/UX Design

Websites, web applications and digital products that explain what a business does, work properly on a phone and make it easy for customers to get in touch or buy.

Book a quick call

Who it helps

Businesses that need a new website, a better one, or a web application their customers or team will use every day.

The problem

Many business websites don’t say clearly what the business does, are awkward to use on a phone, or make it hard to enquire or buy. Visitors leave before they ever get in touch.

What we build

Website design and development
Business websites planned around what visitors need to understand, then designed and built to load quickly on any device.
UI/UX design
User flows, wireframes and interface design that make a website or product easier to understand and easier to use.
Landing pages
Focused pages for a campaign, product or offer, with one clear message and one clear next step.
E-commerce development
Online stores designed around how customers browse, compare and buy, from product page to checkout.
Shopify and WordPress
Sites and stores on platforms your team can update without a developer, set up and extended to fit how you work.
Custom web applications
Portals, booking tools, dashboards and other browser-based software your customers or staff use to get things done.
Responsive front-end development
Layouts built for the phone first and refined for larger screens, so every visitor gets the complete experience.
Redesigns and improvements
A review of an existing site, followed by the changes that make it clearer, faster and easier to use.

How we approach it

What an engagement includes

  • Planning the pages, content and the path a visitor takes
  • UI/UX design for phone, tablet and desktop
  • Development as a custom build, or on WordPress or Shopify if you want to manage it yourself
  • Testing across devices and browsers before launch
  • Launch, and a handover of everything you need to run it

Clarity first

A visitor should understand what you do, who it’s for and what to do next within a few seconds.

Designed for the phone

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone. We design for that screen first, not as an afterthought.

Easy to maintain

Sensible platforms and clean structure, so the site stays easy to update after launch.
