Service 02

Agentic AI Workflows

Connected, AI-powered workflows that coordinate tasks across your tools and take repetitive manual work off your team.

Book a quick call
Who it helps

Businesses where the same manual steps repeat every day: answering enquiries, chasing leads, copying information between tools, preparing updates.

The problem

Work gets stuck between tools and people. Leads wait for a reply, customer questions pile up, and someone spends hours moving information from one place to another.

Services

What we build

  • AI agents for business operations

    Agents that take on recurring tasks such as sorting requests, preparing documents or updating records, with people reviewing where it matters.

  • Multi-agent systems

    Several specialised agents working on a larger process, each responsible for one part, with clear handovers between them.

  • Lead management and follow-up

    New enquiries captured, qualified, routed to the right person and followed up consistently, so fewer leads go cold.

  • Sales and marketing workflows

    Research, outreach preparation, content drafts and reporting, with your team approving what goes out.

  • Customer support workflows

    Incoming questions sorted, answered from your own information where appropriate, and passed to a person when one is needed.

  • Process automation

    Repetitive processes mapped, simplified and automated, so information moves between tools without being re-typed.

  • API and tool integrations

    Connections between your CRM, forms, email, spreadsheets and other tools, so information reaches the place it’s needed.

  • Internal business systems

    Purpose-built tools for a process specific to your business, designed around the way your team already works.

An example: following up new enquiries

  1. 01

    A new enquiry arrives through your website form or email.

  2. 02

    An agent reads it, pulls out the key details and adds them to your CRM or spreadsheet.

  3. 03

    It drafts a reply using your own service and pricing information, and passes it to your team to approve.

  4. 04

    If the customer hasn’t replied after a set time, it prepares a follow-up and reminds the right person.

An illustration of the kind of workflow we build, not a client case.

How we approach it

What an engagement includes

  1. Step 01

    Mapping the current process and where time is being lost

  2. Step 02

    Designing the workflow, including where a person reviews or approves

  3. Step 03

    Building it and connecting it to the tools you already use

  4. Step 04

    Testing it on real cases before it goes live

  5. Step 05

    Documentation, and adjustments once your team is using it
