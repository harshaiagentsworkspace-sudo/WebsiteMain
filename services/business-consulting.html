Business Consulting

Find the bottlenecks. Build a clearer path to growth.

Practical growth and operations consulting. We look at how your business wins customers and gets work done, find what’s holding it back, and set out a clear, prioritised plan.

Talk about your growth strategyBook a quick call
Who it helps

Founders and owners of small and growing businesses who know something isn’t working, but not exactly what, or where to start.

The problem

Growth stalls for reasons that are hard to see from the inside: a sales process that loses enquiries, work that depends on one person, tools that don’t talk to each other, or too many priorities at once.

Services

Where consulting helps

  • Growth assessment

    A structured look at how customers find you, enquire and buy, and where they drop away.

  • Process improvement

    Mapping how work moves through the business, then removing the steps, delays and hand-offs that slow it down.

  • Digital transformation strategy

    Deciding which parts of the business should move to better tools or online channels, in what order and at what cost.

  • Technology and AI adoption

    Choosing the tools and AI uses that fit your business, and planning how your team will start using them.

Common challenges we look at

Most engagements start from a feeling that the business should be doing better than it is. Usually the cause is one of a handful of things.

  • Enquiries come in but too few turn into customers
  • The owner is involved in every decision and every task
  • Work is tracked in inboxes, chats and memory rather than one system
  • Tools have been added one by one and don’t connect
  • There is no shared view of what to work on first

What a strategic assessment covers

We look at the whole path from a customer’s first contact to the work being delivered: how people find and contact you, how enquiries are handled, how work is planned and done, which tools you rely on and where your team’s time goes. The result is a short written report that names the bottlenecks and ranks what to fix.

From plan to implementation

A plan is only useful if it gets done. You can take it to your own team, to another provider, or ask us to carry out the parts we do: a website, a workflow automation or an AI agent. Either way, the plan is yours to keep.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01

What does a consulting engagement involve?

A conversation about your goals, a review of how the business runs today, and a written, prioritised plan. The exact scope and price are agreed in writing before we start.

02

Is business consulting suitable for a small business?

Yes. Small businesses often benefit most, because a few changes in how enquiries and work are handled make a visible difference to a small team.

03

Will you only recommend your own services?

No. If the best fix is a process change, a different tool or another provider, the plan says so.

04

What do we get at the end?

A written plan that names the bottlenecks, ranks what to fix first and sets out practical next steps, plus a conversation to walk through it.

05

Can you help put the plan into action?

Yes, where it involves what we do: websites, workflow automation and AI agents. For anything else, the plan explains what to look for.
