FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01

What does an AI consulting engagement involve?

A conversation about your goals, a review of the workflows and tools involved, and a written, prioritised roadmap. The scope and price are agreed in writing before we start.

02

Do we need data or technical staff in place first?

No. Part of the assessment is understanding what you already have and what each opportunity would need. Many useful automations work with the tools and records a business already keeps.

03

Will you only recommend your own services?

No. If the best option is an off-the-shelf tool, a process change or another provider, the roadmap says so.

04

How do you handle data privacy?

We identify what data each opportunity involves, which providers would process it and under what terms, and recommend controls before anything is connected. We avoid sending data a task doesn’t need.

05

Can you help put the roadmap into action?

Yes, for the parts we build: workflow automation, AI automation and custom AI agents. For anything else, the roadmap explains what to look for.

In more detail

For those who like the full picture

Common starting points

Most engagements start with a sense that AI could help, without a clear view of where.

  • The team is experimenting with AI tools, but nothing is part of daily work yet
  • Repetitive tasks take up hours every week, and it’s unclear which can be automated
  • AI tools have been bought, but they don’t connect to the systems you use
  • There are concerns about data privacy, accuracy or cost that need a clear answer
  • Leadership wants a realistic plan rather than a list of tools

What an AI readiness assessment covers

We look at your key workflows, the tools and data they depend on, and how your team works today. The result is a short written report that names the most promising opportunities, the risks and prerequisites of each, and a recommended order.

Risk and responsible use

AI brings specific risks: incorrect outputs, data sent to third-party providers, dependence on a single vendor, and decisions made without enough oversight. We assess these for each opportunity and recommend controls, such as approval steps, access limits and logging, before anything is built.

From roadmap to implementation and ongoing improvement

A roadmap is only useful if it gets done. You can take it to your own team or another provider, or ask us to implement the parts we build: workflow automation, AI automation and custom AI agents. Once solutions are running, we can review them periodically and refine them as your processes and the available tools change.
