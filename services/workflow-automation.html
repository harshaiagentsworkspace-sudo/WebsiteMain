FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01

What is workflow automation?

Software that carries out the steps of a business process automatically when something happens, such as a form submission, a new email or a scheduled time. It moves information between tools, creates records, sends notifications and triggers the next step.

02

Which tools can you connect?

Most modern business software with an API or built-in integrations can be connected, including many CRMs, email providers, form builders, spreadsheets, calendars and accounting tools. We confirm what’s possible with your specific tools before proposing a solution.

03

Do we need AI for workflow automation?

No. Many valuable workflows are purely rule-based. We add AI only where a step needs reading, classifying or drafting text.

04

What happens if a workflow fails?

It stops, logs the error and alerts the person responsible, rather than carrying on with incomplete data. We design and test these failure cases before launch.

05

Can you improve workflows we already have?

Yes. We can review existing automations, fix fragile steps, add error handling and monitoring, or rebuild them where that is simpler.

In more detail

For those who like the full picture

How this differs from AI automation

Workflow automation handles processes with clear rules: when this happens, do that. It doesn’t need AI to work, which makes it predictable and inexpensive to run. When a step involves reading or interpreting text, such as understanding an email or drafting a reply, we add AI to the workflow. That is what our AI Automation service covers.

Workflow mapping comes first

Before building anything, we document how the process runs today with the people who do it. This often reveals steps that can be removed entirely, which is cheaper than automating them.

  • What starts the process
  • Every step, tool and hand-off
  • The exceptions, and how they are handled today
  • Where delays and errors happen

Error handling and monitoring

Real-world data is messy: a form is missing a field, a service is briefly unavailable, a record already exists. We design for these cases with validation, retries and alerts to a named person when something needs attention. Every run is logged, so you can see what happened and when.

Integrations with the tools you use

We connect tools through their official APIs and integrations, and check that your software supports what the workflow needs before proposing anything. Where a workflow relies on a paid platform, we tell you the running cost up front.
