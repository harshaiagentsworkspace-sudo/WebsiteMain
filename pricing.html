Pricing

A written price before
any work starts

Before we start, you get the full price in writing, with what it covers, what it doesn’t, and when each payment is due. Prices are in Indian rupees.

01

Diagnostic

Fee confirmed before booking

Businesses that know something isn’t working, such as a website that doesn’t bring in enquiries or a process that eats your team’s time, but aren’t sure what to change first.

Included
  • A review of your website, a product flow or one business process
  • A written summary of what is getting in the way, in plain language
  • Prioritised recommendations: what to fix first and what can wait
  • If you want it, a scoped and priced proposal for the work
Not included
  • Design or development work
  • Building or changing a workflow

Larger and custom projects

Quoted per project

Larger websites, e-commerce stores, web applications and AI workflows that need a scope of their own.

Included
  • A written scope that lists what will be built and what won’t
  • Design, development and testing
  • Review points while the work is in progress
  • Launch and handover
Not included

Costs outside our fee

These are paid to the provider, not to Ostendic. Each one that applies to your project is listed in the scope so there are no surprises.

  • Domain name registration and renewal
  • Hosting
  • Platform subscriptions, such as a Shopify plan
  • Paid themes, plugins or apps
  • Usage charges for AI models and other paid APIs

How to start

  1. Send a short description of what you need through the enquiry form, or book a call.
  2. We talk it through and agree what the work should achieve.
  3. You receive a written scope and price. Nothing starts until you agree to it.
